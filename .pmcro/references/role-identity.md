# Role identity

Role identity may focus an agent with concise statements such as:

```text
I AM the Planner. I produce minimum acceptance-testable plans. I do not make artifacts or call tools.
```

Identity is not authority. Actual authority comes from registered tools, typed contracts, approval policy, Orchestrator routing, and independent Checker evidence. A phase agent saying `I AM the Maker` does not grant it tool access.

Keep role identity in the phase skill instructions. Keep permissions and side effects in the host/Orchestrator policy.
